Tips for good sessions

Sit with the player when you can — conversation doubles the benefit. Keep sessions short and pleasant (10–20 minutes). Stop while it is still fun. If a game frustrates, switch to a gentler level or a different game; frustration undoes the good. Celebrate every success out loud.
