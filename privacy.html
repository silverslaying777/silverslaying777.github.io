Privacy Policy

Bright Patch website and apps · Last updated September 1, 2026

The short version

Bright Patch does not collect personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics trackers, and no advertising. What the games remember (difficulty level, sound on or off, sponsor text, whether Complete is unlocked, and your language) is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us.

Payments

On the website, payments are handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe's own pages under Stripe's privacy policy. We receive the name and email address you give Stripe so we can help with your unlock code, and nothing else. If you arrive at the website from one of our advertisements, your device remembers only the name of the ad network so that Stripe can tell us which purchases came from ads.

In the iPhone and iPad app, purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. To confirm which devices have unlocked Bright Patch Complete, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service, which receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and your purchase status from Apple. It receives no name, email, or contact details. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Motion sensors

Water Basketball can optionally use your device's tilt sensor to move the ball. Tilt readings are used only in the moment to move the ball and are never stored or transmitted. The feature is off until you turn it on, and your device asks permission first.

Children

Bright Patch is made for adults living with dementia and their caregivers. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
